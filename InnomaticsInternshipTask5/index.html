Discover Your Career Path

Explore the options and get guidance to advance your career oppurtunities after your +1 and +2 class.

Career Paths

B.E./B.Tech (Bachelor of Engineering/Bachelor of Technology):

This is the most popular choice for MPC students. Specializations include:

  • Computer Science Engineering
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Electrical and Electronics Engineering
  • Civil Engineering
  • Electronics and Communication Engineering
  • Chemical Engineering
  • Information Technology
  • Aerospace Engineering
  • Artificial Intelligence and Data Science

Integrated M.Tech:

5-year integrated Master's degree in engineering fields.

Diploma in Engineering:

For students looking to take a shorter path into technical fields.

B.Arch (Bachelor of Architecture)

A 5-year program for students interested in architecture, design, and planning.

B.Sc. (Bachelor of Science)

This is suitable for students who are interested in pursuing a career in science and research. Specializations include:

  • Mathematics
  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • Computer Science
  • Electronics
  • Data Science
  • Statistics

Integrated M.Sc.:

A 5-year program leading to a Master's degree in Science.

\

BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications)

A 3-year program for students interested in computer programming, software development, and IT fields.

BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration)

Suitable for students interested in business, management, and entrepreneurship.

B.Com (Bachelor of Commerce)

For students inclined towards commerce, accounting, finance, and economics.

BMS (Bachelor of Management Studies)

A management-focused undergraduate program.

Integrated Law Courses

For example, **B.Sc. LLB** or **BBA LLB**, which combine science or business with law over a 5-year period.

NDA (National Defence Academy)

Students can apply for the NDA exam to join the Indian Army, Navy, or Air Force.

Merchant Navy

A career in maritime studies, with courses like B.Sc. in Nautical Science, Marine Engineering, etc.

Commercial Pilot Training

For students interested in becoming a pilot.

B.Sc. in Aviation

Academic training in aviation, including airport management and flight operations.

B.Des (Bachelor of Design)

Specializations include product design, industrial design, fashion design, etc.

BFA (Bachelor of Fine Arts)

For students interested in arts, graphic design, or animation.

BHM (Bachelor of Hotel Management)

For students interested in hospitality, tourism, and hotel management.

B.Ed (Bachelor of Education)

A degree for students interested in becoming teachers at the school level.

B.Sc. B.Ed. (Integrated Program)

A 4-year integrated program in education and science.

About Us

Welcome to EduPath2Sucess, a dedicated platform designed to guide students in the MPC (Maths, Physics, Chemistry) stream as they navigate their journey toward higher education and exciting career opportunities. Our mission is to provide students with clear, concise, and reliable information about the wide array of career paths available after 10+2. Whether you're aiming for engineering, pure sciences, architecture, or research fields, we’re here to help you make informed decisions about your future.

At EduPath2Sucess, we offer:

  • Comprehensive Career Guidance: Detailed information on various career options, including engineering, technology, research, and government services.
  • Higher Education Opportunities: Insights into the top courses, entrance exams, and universities for MPC students in India and abroad.
  • Expert Tips and Resources: Study materials, exam preparation strategies, and expert advice to help you excel in your chosen path.
  • Interactive Tools: Personalized career quizzes and tools to help you discover courses that align with your interests and strengths.
We understand the challenges MPC students face when choosing their next steps, and our goal is to make this process easier by providing accurate and up-to-date information on every relevant aspect. From JEE and NEET preparation to exploring non-traditional careers, we've got you covered. Start exploring today and set your sights on a bright and successful future!
